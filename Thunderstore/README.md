# Ullr's Peak: Mythic Ski & Snowboard

Shred Valheim's mountains with your friends using mythic frost-rune skis, drakkar snowboards, backcountry jump kickers, timed slalom race gates, and chained uphill rope tows!

## Gear (Workbench Level 1)
- **Ullr's Frost-Rune Skis** (`6 Wood` + `2 LeatherScraps`) — Twin carved ash & boreal silver skis with glowing cyan runes and **Skadi's Runic Ski Poles**.
- **Moder's Drakkar Snowboard** (`6 Wood` + `2 LeatherScraps`) — Wide twin-tip snowboard with sideways carve stance, tighter turn radius, and big bunny-hop pop.
- **Ullr's Frost Ward** — Equipping either item in your **Utility** slot grants **Frost Resistance** so you and your friends can head straight up a mountain without freezing, and automatically marks **Ullr's Summit** (the tallest nearby mountain peak) on your minimap!

## Controls
- **`[X]`** *(configurable)* — Click into / unclip from your Skis or Snowboard (or simply sprint onto a snowy slope!). Equipping your **Hammer** automatically unclips so you can build safely on steep slopes.
- **`[A]` / `[D]` + Mouse Look** — Carve turns (edge bite converts lateral slip into curved downhill speed).
- **`[W]`** — **Aero Downhill Tuck** at high speed, or **Pole-Push / Skate-Push** on flat ground & gentle uphills.
- **`[S]`** — **Snowplow / Hockey-Stop Brake** with powder rooster-tail spray.
- **`[Space]`** — Hold briefly & release for a crouch-charged **Bunny-Hop** (or hop off a Rope Tow).
- **`[A]` / `[D]` + `[Right-Click / C]` (Mid-Air)** — **180° / 360° / 540°+ Spins** and **Frost Grabs** (clean stomps award a landing speed boost!).

## Buildable Mountain Park Extras (`Hammer` -> `Ullr's Peak` tab, no Workbench needed!)
1. **Ullr's Frost-Rune Kicker Ramp** (`8 Wood`) — Curved parabolic launch ramp with frost boost runes.
2. **Ullr's Slalom Race Gate** (`4 Wood`) — Press **`[E]`** to cycle between **Start**, **Checkpoint**, and **Finish** gates. Crossing a Finish Gate broadcasts your official run time and peak km/h speed to everyone!
3. **Ullr's Runic Rope Tow** (`10 Wood` + `2 LeatherScraps`) — Place two or more pylons uphill (up to `85m` per span, chainable all the way up a 480m summit!). Press **`[E]`** on a pylon or near the glowing frost cable to ride smoothly back to the top!
